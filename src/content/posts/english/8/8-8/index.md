### 今日核心词汇梳理
1.  **词根词缀拓展**：围绕前缀*bene-（表示“好”）*拓展词汇：beneficial（有益的）、benefit（益处；使受益）、beneficiary（受益人）
2.  **形近词辨析**：区分`compliment`（恭维，赞美）与`complement`（补充，补足），搭配练习：pay a compliment to sb. / complement sth.
3.  **熟词僻义积累**：address 除常见义“地址”外，还可表示“演讲；处理，解决”，例句：The government is addressing the housing crisis.
### 高频搭配整理：
-  adhere to 坚持，例句：She adhered to her original plan despite the objections.
-  in terms of 在……方面，例句：In terms of language proficiency, he is among the top students.

## 长难句拆解练习
> 原句：Studies from the University of California show that employees who have access to flexible working arrangements report higher levels of job satisfaction and lower rates of burnout.
> 结构分析：主句为`Studies from the University of California show that...`，宾语从句中包含定语从句`who have access to flexible working arrangements`修饰先行词employees，嵌套了并列的宾语成分`higher levels of job satisfaction and lower rates of burnout`。
> 参考翻译：加州大学的研究表明，能够享受灵活工作安排的员工报告称自身工作满意度更高，职业倦怠率更低。
