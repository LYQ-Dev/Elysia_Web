Today I reviewed 4 high-frequency core words for the postgraduate exam, with key collocations and example sentences for each:
1.  **permeate** /ˈpɜːmieɪt/ v. 弥漫，遍布；渗透
    - Common collocation for PET: `permeate through` 渗透过
    - Example: The influence of digital technology has permeated almost every corner of contemporary society.
2.  **allege** /əˈledʒ/ v. （未经证实地）断言，宣称；指控
    - PET高频易混辨析：Distinguished from `declare` (正式宣告) and `proclaim` (公开宣布), allege emphasizes unproven claims
    - Example: The local factory was alleged to have discharged unprocessed waste into the river.
3.  **tangible** /ˈtændʒəbl/ adj. 切实的，有形的；可触摸的
    - Common collocation: `tangible benefits/progress` 切实利益/进展
    - Example: The new educational reform has brought tangible changes to rural schools.
4.  **concomitant** /kənˈkɒmɪtənt/ adj. 伴随的，共存的 n. 伴随物
    - Common collocation: `concomitant with` 与……相伴
    - Example: Rapid urbanization is concomitant with a series of social challenges.
