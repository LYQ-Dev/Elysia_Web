### 今日重点考研高频词
1.  **perceive** /pəˈsiːv/ 
    - 词义：v. 察觉，感知；理解，领悟
    - 常用搭配：perceive sth. as sth. 把…看作；认为
    - 真题例句：Most consumers perceive green products as more expensive than traditional ones.
2.  **discern** /dɪˈsɜːn/
    - 词义：v. 识别，辨认出；洞悉，理解
    - 常用搭配：discern between right and wrong 明辨是非
3.  **mitigate** /ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/
    - 词义：v. 减轻，缓解（伤害、问题等）
    - 常用搭配：mitigate the impact of 减轻…的影响
4.  **concomitant** /kənˈkɒmɪtənt/
    - 词义：adj. 伴随的，随之而来的；n. 伴随物

## 长难句拆解
### 今日真题长难句
> 原句：The most famous of these efforts was initiated by the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), which has a yearly turnover of more than £100 million, and its two well-known theatres, the Royal Shakespeare Theatre and the Swan Theatre.
> 
> 拆分分析：
> 1.  主句主干：The most famous of these efforts was initiated by the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC)
> 2.  非限制性定语从句修饰先行词RSC：which has a yearly turnover of more than £100 million
> 3.  并列补充说明剧团旗下资产：and its two well-known theatres, the Royal Shakespeare Theatre and the Swan Theatre.
> 
> 参考译文：这些举措中最知名的一项由皇家莎士比亚剧团（RSC）发起，该剧团年营业额超过1亿英镑，旗下拥有两家知名剧院：皇家莎士比亚剧院和天鹅剧院。
