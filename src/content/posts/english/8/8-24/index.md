### 核心考纲词汇梳理
#### 形近词组1：以-ceive结尾
1. **conceive** /kənˈsiːv/ v. 构思，设想；怀孕
   例句：It is difficult to conceive of a plan to end poverty. 很难想出一个消除贫困的计划。
2. **perceive** /pəˈsiːv/ v. 察觉，感知；理解
   例句：Most people perceive him as a tough businessman. 大多数人认为他是一个强硬的商人。
3. **deceive** /dɪˈsiːv/ v. 欺骗，蒙骗
   例句：He deceived his friends about his income. 他在自己的收入问题上欺骗了朋友。

## 长难句拆解
选自2023年考研英语一Text 3 原句：
> "The potential evolution of today's technology, and its social consequences, is as dazzling as the evolution of human culture, which took place over tens of thousands of years."

### 结构拆解
1. 主句主干：The potential evolution... and its social consequences is as dazzling as the evolution of human culture
   - 主语是两个并列的名词短语，谓语动词用单数is，因为主语被视为整体概念
   - 比较状语：as dazzling as the evolution of human culture
2. 定语从句：which took place over tens of thousands of years，修饰先行词the evolution of human culture

### 参考翻译：当今技术的潜在发展及其社会影响，如同历经数万年演变的人类文化发展一样令人炫目。
