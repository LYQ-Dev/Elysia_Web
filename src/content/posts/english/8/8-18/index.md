## 长难句拆解（选自2023年考研英语一Text 2）
> 原句：The industry’s approach to addressing this momentary crisis is not only to rely on market forces, but also to adjust to the fact that consumers’ attitudes toward energy are shifting.
1.  **语法拆分**：
    - 主句主语：The industry’s approach；后置定语：to addressing this momentary crisis
    - 主句谓语：is not only...but also... 平行结构，后接两个不定式作表语
    - 同位语从句：that consumers’ attitudes toward energy are shifting，修饰先行词the fact，解释说明事实内容
2.  **参考翻译**：该行业应对这一短期危机的方法不仅是依靠市场力量，还需适应“消费者对能源的态度正在转变”这一现状。
