    - 真题语境例句：The politician’s ambiguous statement left voters uncertain about his policy priorities.
    - 搭配拓展：ambiguous wording/instructions 措辞含糊/指令不明
2.  **conspicuous** /kənˈspɪkjuəs/ adj. 显著的，显眼的
    - 真题语境例句：The new environmental regulations have made conspicuous changes to local industrial production.
    - 词根记忆：con-(together) + spic(see) + -uous → 被众人看到的 → 显著的
3.  **deduce** /dɪˈdjuːs/ v. 推断，演绎
    - 真题语境例句：Researchers deduced the ancient climate from the chemical composition of the fossil samples.
    - 派生词：deduction n. 推理，演绎；扣除额
4.  **stagnant** /ˈstæɡnənt/ adj. 停滞的，不景气的
    - 真题语境例句：The stagnant global trade has impacted small export-oriented enterprises severely.
    - 近义词辨析：stagnant（侧重发展停滞）vs. stationary（侧重静止不动）
5.  **emulate** /ˈemjuleɪt/ v. 仿效，赶超
    - 真题语境例句：Many young graduates emulate successful entrepreneurs to start their own businesses.
    - 熟词僻义：可表示“与…竞争，匹敌”

## 长难句专项训练
### 真题长难句（2024年考研英语一Text 3）
> The survey found that only 18 percent of small businesses have access to low-interest loans, which is far lower than the government’s promised coverage rate of 40 percent.
1.  **结构拆分**
    - 主句：The survey found + that引导的宾语从句
    - 宾语从句：only 18 percent of small businesses have access to low-interest loans + which引导的非限制性定语从句（修饰前面的18%数据）
2.  精准翻译：该调查发现，仅有18%的小企业能够获得低息贷款，这远低于政府承诺的40%的覆盖比例。
3.  重点搭配：have access to 意为“有权使用，有机会获得”，是考研高频必背短语。
