## 长难句解析（选自2011年英语一Text3）
> The researchers studied the behaviour of female brown capuchin monkeys, which have the same emotions, genetics and social structures as humans.
### 句子拆解
1.  主句：`The researchers studied the behaviour of female brown capuchin monkeys`
    主语：The researchers；谓语：studied；宾语：the behaviour，后置定语`of female brown capuchin monkeys`修饰behaviour
2.  非限制性定语从句：`which have the same emotions, genetics and social structures as humans`，关系代词which指代先行词monkeys
