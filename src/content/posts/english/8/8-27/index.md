> 1.  句首状语：Rather than concluding that..., 意为“并未断言……”，其中that引导宾语从句作concluding的宾语
> 2.  主句主干：Senior suggests [宾语从句]
> 3.  宾语从句主干：we need to redefine happiness，冒号后内容对“重新定义幸福”进行具体解释
> 4.  解释部分：instead of thinking of it as something that..., we should consider being happy as a past-tense condition，其中that引导定语从句修饰something
