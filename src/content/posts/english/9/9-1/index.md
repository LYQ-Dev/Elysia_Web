##  Core Vocabulary Review (AI sorted high-frequency postgraduate entrance exam words)
1.  **accumulate** /əˈkjuːmjəleɪt/ v. 积累，积聚；逐步获得（知识、技能）
    - 常用搭配：accumulate wealth/experience/knowledge
    - 真题例句：Many people accumulate wealth through long-term rational investment.
2.  **consecutive** /kənˈsekjətɪv/ adj. 连续的，连贯的；接连不断的
    - 常用搭配：consecutive years/consecutive quarters
    - 写作用法：The domestic film market has seen box office growth for 7 consecutive years.
3.  **depict** /dɪˈpɪkt/ v. 描绘，描述；刻画（人物/场景/社会现象）
    - 真题搭配：depict the rural social phenomenon
    - 例句：The documentary depicts the real lives of homeless people.
4.  **feasible** /ˈfiːzəbl/ adj. 可行的，可实现的
    - 写作用法：put forward a feasible solution
    - 例句：The proposed environmental policy is technically feasible.
5.  **hence** /hens/ adv. 因此，所以；由此
    - 衔接技巧：替代口语化的so，用于正式写作和翻译
    - 例句：The survey data is incomplete, hence the research conclusion is not persuasive.
