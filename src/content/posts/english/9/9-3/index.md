> 🔍 结构拆解：
> - 主句主干：The most striking finding ... is that + 表语从句
> - 表语从句嵌套两个时间/让步状语从句：`even when individuals recognize their own implicit biases` 和 `particularly when they are operating under tight deadlines or cognitive load`
> - 固定句式：`find it + adj. + to do sth.` 表示“发现做某事是……的”
