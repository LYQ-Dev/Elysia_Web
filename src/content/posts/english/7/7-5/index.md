> 原句：The target is wrong, for in attacking the standards, critics divert attention from the fault that lies with ill-informed or incompetent users.
### 结构拆分
1.  主句：`The target is wrong`（主系表结构）
2.  原因状语从句：`for in attacking the standards, critics divert attention from the fault`，其中`in attacking the standards`为介词短语作状语
3.  定语从句：`that lies with ill-informed or incompetent users`，修饰先行词`the fault`，关系代词that在从句中作主语
