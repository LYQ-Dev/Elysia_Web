## 长难句分析
> 原句（2025英语一Text 3）：Yet rather than embracing a new paradigm of sustainable development, many corporations continue to prioritize short-term profits over long-term environmental protection, a trend that has been exacerbated by the pressure of quarterly earnings reports.
> 结构拆解：
> 1.  主句主干：many corporations continue to prioritize short-term profits over long-term environmental protection
> 2.  状语：rather than embracing a new paradigm of sustainable development （否定式比较状语）
> 3.  同位语：a trend 指代前文提到的“将短期利润置于环保之上”的现象，其后接定语从句that has been exacerbated by the pressure of quarterly earnings reports 补充说明该趋势的加剧原因。
> 参考翻译：然而，许多企业并未拥抱可持续发展的新范式，反而将短期利润置于长期环境保护之上，季度财报的考核压力更是加剧了这一不良趋势。
