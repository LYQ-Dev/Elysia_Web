1.  **adhere [ədˈhɪə(r)]** v. 粘附；坚持，追随
    考研搭配：`adhere to`（正式语境下坚持原则、协议等），例句：*All parties must adhere to the peace agreement.*
    易混辨析：与`stick to`的区别，`stick to`多用于日常坚持计划、观点，`adhere to`更偏向官方、正式的承诺与规则。
2.  **conventional [kənˈvenʃənl]** adj. 传统的，惯例的；常规的
    真题例句：*Conventional wisdom says that more choice is better.*
3.  **discrepancy [dɪˈskrepənsi]** n. 差异，不一致（尤指数据、说法间的矛盾）
    搭配：`discrepancy between A and B`，例句：*There is a discrepancy between the two reports.*
4.  **evoke [ɪˈvəʊk]** v. 唤起，引起（情感、记忆）
    真题搭配：`evoke strong reactions`，例句：*The speech evoked heated debate among the audience.*
5.  **inherent [ɪnˈhɪərənt]** adj. 固有的，内在的
    例句：*There is an inherent risk in any new investment.*
