## 长难句分析

> The researchers from the University of California and Stanford University, who analyzed data from more than 10,000 adult participants, found that people who slept fewer than six hours per night were more likely to develop chronic diseases.

### 结构拆分
1. 主句主干：The researchers... found that...
2. 非限定性定语从句：`who analyzed data from more than 10,000 adult participants`，修饰先行词`The researchers`
3. 宾语从句主干：`people... were more likely to develop chronic diseases`
4. 定语从句嵌套：`who slept fewer than six hours per night`，修饰先行词`people`
