## 长难句拆解
### 难句1
> 原句：The researchers argue that a lack of formal language learning, which may be common in families where parents are highly educated, does not necessarily mean that children will fail to develop a sophisticated sense of grammar.
> 拆分解析：
> 1.  主句：The researchers argue + 宾语从句
> 2.  宾语从句主干：a lack of formal language learning does not necessarily mean that...
> 3.  非限制性定语从句：which may be common in families 修饰a lack of formal language learning
> 4.  定语从句嵌套定语从句：where parents are highly educated 修饰families
> 5.  最终翻译：研究人员认为，即便在父母受过高等教育的家庭中普遍缺乏正式的语言学习，也并不意味着孩子们无法形成复杂的语法意识。
### 难句2
> 原句：It is this apparent disconnect between educational philosophy and classroom practice that helps explain why the system has failed to produce the kind of critical thinkers that policymakers claim to desire.
> 拆分解析：强调句型It is...that，核心是this apparent disconnect helps explain why...，后续嵌套宾语从句和定语从句，翻译时调整语序突出强调部分。
