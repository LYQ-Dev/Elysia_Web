> 原句：Do you remember all those years when scientists argued that smoking would kill us but the doubters insisted that we didn't know for sure? That the evidence was inconclusive, the science uncertain?
1.  **语法结构拆解**：
    - 主句：Do you remember all those years
    - 定语从句：`when scientists argued that...but the doubters insisted that...` 修饰先行词`years`
