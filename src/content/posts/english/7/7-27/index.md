## 长难句分析
今日选取2023年考研英语一阅读真题的两个经典长难句进行专项练习：
### 句子1
> 原句：Rather than dismissing the trend as a passing fad, scholars argue that it reflects a fundamental shift in how modern societies value intellectual labor.
> 🔍 结构拆解：
> 1.  让步状语：`Rather than dismissing the trend as a passing fad`，固定搭配`dismiss sth as...`意为“将…斥为…”
> 2.  主句：`scholars argue that...`
> 3.  宾语从句：`it reflects a fundamental shift in how modern societies value intellectual labor`，其中`how modern societies value intellectual labor`为介词in的宾语从句
> 📝 参考翻译：学者们并未将这一趋势斥为一时的潮流，而是认为它反映了现代社会对脑力劳动价值认知的根本转变。

### 句子2
> 原句：The data collected from over 5,000 participants, which was published in the journal *Nature Human Behaviour*, has sparked widespread debate among sociologists.
> 🔍 结构拆解：
> 1.  主语：`The data collected from over 5,000 participants`，过去分词短语`collected from over 5,000 participants`作后置定语修饰data
> 2.  非限制性定语从句：`which was published in the journal *Nature Human Behaviour*`，修饰先行词the data
> 3.  主句谓语和宾语：`has sparked widespread debate among sociologists`
> 📝 参考翻译：这份收集自5000余名参与者的数据发表于《自然·人类行为》期刊，引发了社会学家们的广泛讨论。
