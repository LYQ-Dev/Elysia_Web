5.  **depict** /dɪˈpɪkt/ v. 描绘，描述；刻画
    - 考研语境例句：The novel vividly depicts the struggles of a young immigrant family navigating life in a new country.
6.  **易错词辨析：complimentary vs complementary**
    - complimentary：adj. 免费的；赞美的，恭维的
      例句：The hotel offered complimentary breakfast to all overnight guests.
    - complementary：adj. 互补的，补充的
      例句：The two training programs are complementary and together cover all aspects of professional development.
7.  **persist vs insist**
    - persist：v. 坚持（行为），持续存在，后接in
      例句：Despite repeated warnings, he persisted in skipping his morning classes.
    - insist：v. 坚持（观点、要求），后接on
      例句：The doctor insisted that the patient get a full blood test.
