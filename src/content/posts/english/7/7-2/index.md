### 重点词汇精讲
1.  **perspective** /pəˈspektɪv/
    - 核心释义：n. 视角，观点；透视画法
    - 考研高频搭配：from one's perspective 从某人的视角出发；put sth in perspective 正确看待某事
    - 真题同源例句：We need to adopt a long-term perspective when addressing climate change.（摘自2022年英语一Text 3）
2.  **constitute** /ˈkɒnstɪtjuːt/
    - 核心释义：v. 组成，构成；设立，制定
    - 形近词辨析：
      - constituent n. 组成部分；选民 adj. 组成的
      - constitutional adj. 宪法的，符合宪法的
    - 真题例句：These outdated regulations constitute a barrier to international trade.（2021年英语二Text 2）
3.  **deteriorate** /dɪˈtɪəriəreɪt/
    - 核心释义：v. 恶化，变坏（侧重渐进式的变坏过程）
    - 近义词对比：worsen 更强调结果性的恶化，deteriorate 更突出过程
    - 真题同源句：Public health services have deteriorated rapidly in rural areas over the past decade.

## 长难句分析
### 今日拆解长难句1
> The researchers studied the behaviour of female brown capuchin monkeys, which have been shown to possess a sense of fairness that is remarkably similar to that of humans.
1.  结构拆分：
    - 主句主干：The researchers studied the behaviour
    - 后置定语：of female brown capuchin monkeys 修饰behaviour
    - 非限制性定语从句1：which have been shown to possess a sense of fairness 修饰monkeys
    - 嵌套定语从句2：that is remarkably similar to that of humans 修饰a sense of fairness，其中第二个that指代前文的a sense of fairness
2.  难点突破：理清多层定语从句的指代关系，避免混淆修饰对象
3.  标准翻译：研究人员对雌性棕色卷尾猴的行为展开了研究，这些猴子已被证实拥有与人类极为相似的公平感知能力。

### 今日拆解长难句2
> Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit, we can instead direct our own change by consciously developing new habits.
1.  结构拆分：
    - 让步状语：Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit
    - 主句：we can instead direct our own change by consciously developing new habits，其中by引导方式状语
2.  重点搭配：dismiss sb/sth as... 认为…（不值得认真对待）；develop habits 培养习惯
3.  翻译参考：与其将自己视作无法改变的习惯产物，不如通过有意识地培养新习惯来掌控自身的改变。
