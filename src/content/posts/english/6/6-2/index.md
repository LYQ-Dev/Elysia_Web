## 长难句分析
### 长难句1（2025英语一Text 3）
> The researchers found that the proportion of people who felt happy with their lives rose from 32% in 2012 to 42% in 2023, when the economy finally began to recover.
- 语法拆解：主句为`The researchers found that...`，宾语从句中包含定语从句`who felt happy with their lives`修饰先行词`people`，句末`when`引导非限制性定语从句修饰`2023`。
- 参考译文：研究人员发现，对生活感到满意的人群比例从2012年的32%升至2023年的42%，彼时经济终于开始复苏。

### 长难句2（2025英语一Text 3）
> Flexible working arrangements, which have become increasingly popular in recent years, allow employees to better balance their professional and personal lives.
- 语法拆解：主句为`Flexible working arrangements allow employees to better balance...`，中间`which`引导非限制性定语从句修饰先行词`Flexible working arrangements`。
- 参考译文：近年来愈发流行的弹性工作安排，能够让员工更好地平衡职业与个人生活。
