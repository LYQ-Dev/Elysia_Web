### 今日易错点复盘
今天混淆了**compose**与**comprise**的用法：
- compose：表示“组成”，常用被动结构`be composed of`（由…组成）
- comprise：表示“包含、由…组成”，本身已包含被动含义，无需使用被动语态

## 长难句拆解
今日拆解的经典长难句来自2010年英语一Text 3：
> *"In his book The Tipping Point, Malcolm Gladwell argues that "social epidemics" are driven in large part by the actions of a tiny minority of special individuals, often called influentials, who are unusually informed, persuasive, or well connected."*

### 语法拆解
1.  主句结构：Malcolm Gladwell argues + that引导的宾语从句
2.  宾语从句主干：`"social epidemics" are driven... by the actions of a tiny minority of special individuals`
3.  后置定语：`often called influentials` 是过去分词短语，修饰special individuals
4.  非限制性定语从句：`who are unusually informed, persuasive, or well connected`，进一步修饰special individuals
