## 长难句拆解训练
今日重点拆解了2025年英语一阅读Text 2中的嵌套长难句：
> *The researchers found that, among the 2,300 parents surveyed, those who regularly read bedtime stories to their children had significantly higher scores on measures of emotional well-being than those who did not.*
### 拆分步骤：
1.  定位主句主干：`The researchers found that + 宾语从句`
2.  解析宾语从句：主干为`those had significantly higher scores`，其中`among the 2,300 parents surveyed`作地点状语，`who regularly read bedtime stories to their children`为定语从句修饰先行词`those`
3.  识别比较状语：`than those who did not`，其中`did`指代前文的`had read bedtime stories`
今日收获：熟练掌握了嵌套定语从句的拆分逻辑，能够快速剥离修饰成分定位句子核心含义。
