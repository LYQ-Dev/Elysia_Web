### 今日拆解例句（2020年英语一Text3）
> 原句：Instead of including that paragraph, she added one that described Lengel's crabbed response to the girls so that she could lead up to the A & P "policy" he enforced.
> 语法拆解：主句为`she added one`，前面的`Instead of including that paragraph`为介词短语作让步状语；`that`引导定语从句修饰先行词one；`so that`引导目的状语从句，其中`he enforced`为省略关系代词that的定语从句修饰先行词policy。
