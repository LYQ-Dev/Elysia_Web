### 拆解例句（选自2018英一Text 3）
> Original Sentence: "The question that has puzzled economists and policymakers alike is how exactly the digital transformation of the world’s largest economy is reshaping the way we work, live and interact."

#### 语法拆解
1.  主句核心结构：`The question [定语从句] is [表语从句]`
2.  定语从句：`that has puzzled economists and policymakers alike`，修饰先行词`the question`，其中`alike`为副词，意为“同样地，两者都”，修饰`economists and policymakers`
3.  表语从句：`how exactly the digital transformation of the world’s largest economy is reshaping the way we work, live and interact`
    - 从句主语：`the digital transformation of the world’s largest economy`
    - 谓语：`is reshaping`
    - 宾语：`the way`，其后`we work, live and interact`为省略了`that/in which`的定语从句，修饰`the way`

#### 易错反思
一开始误将`alike`理解为仅修饰`policymakers`，通过查阅语法笔记明确：`alike`通常放在名词/代词后，修饰两者及以上的并列对象，此处修饰`economists and policymakers`。
