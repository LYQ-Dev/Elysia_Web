## 长难句分析
选取2025英一Text3的经典长难句为例：
> The most glaring flaw of the social cure as it’s presented is that it doesn’t work well for a long time, unlike most advertising or public-health campaigns, which have been proven to have lasting effects.
### 语法拆解
1.  主句主干：The most glaring flaw ... is that + 表语从句
2.  `as it’s presented` 为定语从句，修饰先行词`the social cure`，意为“正如所呈现的社会治愈方案”
3.  句末`which have been proven to have lasting effects`为非限定性定语从句，修饰`most advertising or public-health campaigns`
### 参考翻译
正如所呈现的那样，社会治愈方案最明显的缺陷在于：它无法长期发挥作用，而大多数广告或公共卫生活动已被证明能产生持久的效果。
